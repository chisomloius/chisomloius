## About Me

I am a **Senior BI Developer, Data Engineer, Solution Architect, and Digital Transformation professional** with 8+ years of experience across business intelligence, enterprise application development, operational reporting, data validation, analytics automation, and stakeholder engagement.

I design and deliver solutions across the full lifecycle, from refining an early business request and defining KPI logic to modelling data, building applications and dashboards, establishing validation controls, supporting deployment, and improving adoption.

I am especially interested in creating reliable platforms that improve decision-making, reduce manual work, strengthen governance, and make technology easier for people to use.

## How I Work

```text
Discover → Clarify → Architect → Build → Validate → Review → Deploy → Measure → Improve
```

1. **Discover the real problem** behind the initial request.
2. **Translate business goals** into requirements, KPIs, acceptance criteria, and delivery priorities.
3. **Design for scale and maintainability**, not only for the first release.
4. **Build with validation and governance** incorporated from the start.
5. **Use peer review** for calculation logic, security-sensitive changes, and production releases.
6. **Measure adoption and outcomes** after deployment.
7. **Document decisions** so teams can support and improve the solution.
